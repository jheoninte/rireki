Privacy policy

Updated November 20, 2026

Your draft

Guest drafts are stored in this browser’s localStorage. Clearing site data or closing a private browsing session may remove them. English, Japanese and Korean drafts are separate. A pasted job description is part of the saved draft but is excluded from document downloads.

Account and cloud storage

When account services are connected, signing in uses Supabase authentication. If you choose cloud storage, your draft is stored under your account with account access controls. We do not automatically send resumes to employers. Browser storage is not end-to-end encrypted.

Writing tools and exports

English writing checks, keyword comparison and document generation run in your browser. The English editor does not send your writing to an AI service. Fonts are served from this site. PDF and Word files are downloaded to your device.

External services and deletion

External job links open the provider’s service without automatically attaching your resume. Use the editor’s cloud deletion control to remove a cloud draft. Clear this site’s browser data to remove local drafts and backups. For account requests, contact jheoninte@gmail.com from your registered email; do not attach passwords or resume files.
